About us

The Precision Medicine Statistics (PMS) Working Group was approved by the American Statistical Association Biopharmaceutical Section (ASA BIOP) in April 2025. Our goal is to promote harmonized, transparent statistical methods for developing, validating, and using biomarkers and companion diagnostics, so that rigorous evidence supports decision-making and personalized therapies reach patients more efficiently.
